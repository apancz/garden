people
in a crowd
stand like trees
finger branches
send signals
sickness ripple

to the bugs
who just moved to the city
we look like forests 
or perhaps
more like lamp posts
lantern phone lights
attractive to them
they don’t know any better
climb our buildings 
ride the subway 
wait for their stop 
patiently 
jersey to columbus circle
*this is me!*
stepping out
they walk right under the turnstile
moths fly overhead  
these damn free loaders
what happened to this city ?
squish! 
and squish again
to be sure
but the bugs keep coming
so why squish 
under my shoe
when I could try walking in theirs
see how our bodies
really do look like trees
from below
